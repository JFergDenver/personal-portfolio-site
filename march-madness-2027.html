what the columns mean
AdjOE
Points scored per 100 possessions, adjusted for opponent. Higher is better.
AdjDE
Points allowed per 100 possessions, adjusted for opponent. Lower is better.
Net
AdjOE minus AdjDE.
Barthag
Torvik's estimated chance of beating an average Division I team.
WAB
Wins above bubble: results so far compared with what a typical bubble team would have against this schedule.
SOS
Sports-Reference strength of schedule in points per game. Positive means a tougher schedule.
AP, BPI
Latest AP poll rank and ESPN BPI rank. ESPN lists only its top 50, so lower-rated teams show >50.
Tempo
Torvik's adjusted possessions per 40 minutes.
